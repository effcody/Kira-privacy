# Privacy Policy for Kira Music

**Last Updated: 24/11/2026**

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how Kira Music ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects information when used on Discord servers. By using Kira, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Data We Collect
To provide its music services, Kira may access or process the following information:

- **User Information:** Discord User IDs, usernames, and other basic identifiers to recognise users and process commands.
- **Server Data:** Server IDs and channel IDs to manage music playback and server-specific settings.
- **Voice Data:** Voice channel and session information required to connect Kira and play music.
- **Command Data:** Slash command interactions and options, such as requested song names, URLs, and playback settings.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
The information accessed by Kira is used for the following purposes:

- **Music Playback:** To search for, queue, and play requested music.
- **Command Execution:** To process commands such as `/play`, `/queue`, `/autoplay`, and other music controls.
- **Server Management:** To maintain server-specific music settings and preferences.
- **Service Improvement:** To troubleshoot errors, maintain performance, and improve the Bot's reliability.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
Kira only retains information necessary to provide its services.

- **Music Requests:** Song queries and playback information are processed to provide music features.
- **Server Settings:** Any saved server configurations are retained while needed to provide the relevant features.
- **Temporary Data:** Queue and playback session information may be held temporarily and cleared when the session ends or the Bot restarts.
- **Data Deletion:** Stored server-related data can be removed when the Bot is removed from a server, subject to backup and technical retention periods.

## 5. Data Sharing
Kira does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide music search, audio streaming, hosting, or other Bot functionality. Such services may process information under their own privacy policies.

## 6. User Rights
Users and server administrators may request information about stored data or request its deletion by contacting the Kira development team through the official Support Server.

Server administrators may also remove Kira from their server to stop its services. Removal does not necessarily guarantee immediate deletion of backups or records retained for legitimate technical purposes.

## 7. Contact Information
If you have questions, concerns, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the Kira development team through our official Support Server.
